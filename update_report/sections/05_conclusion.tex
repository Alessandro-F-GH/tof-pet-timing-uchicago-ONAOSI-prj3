\section{Conclusion}

This update presents a complete waveform-analysis chain for ML-based timing correction in TOF-PET detectors. The analysis begins from oscilloscope TRC files, preserves the raw acquisition in ROOT format, applies physically motivated event selection, constructs an LED-referenced waveform dataset, and evaluates a shared antisymmetric locally connected model on blind data.

The central modeling choice is to combine local temporal connectivity with position-specific weights. This retains local waveform structure while avoiding the translation-weight-sharing assumption of a CNN for signals that have already been aligned to a physical timing reference.
