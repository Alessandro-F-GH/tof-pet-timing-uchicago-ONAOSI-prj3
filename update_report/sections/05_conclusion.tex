\section{Conclusion}

The model leads to a consistent improvement for both modes, even if the biggest improvement is observed when applying the method
to the energy channel data. Also, the improvement on energy channel is mostly determined by the first rising part of the signal: the ctr
doesn't improve significantly increasing the window length and the xai analysiss shows a single sharp peak in importance on the first edge only
for both boards data. The relative improvement is consistent with previous work and the LED correction is unbiased, as observed by Onishi 2022 (ref). 
The application of ML to the timing channel is novel to the best of our knowledge. Since the timing channel is already designed for timing, producing
very similar sharp signals, it is expected that the LED error that can be corrected using the waveform shape is reduced. Snyway, it is interesting
that the improvement is significantly and consistently increased  using a bigger window (both boards), and XAI shows that relevant timing information can be found
even 20 ns after the arrival. This effect suggest that both the leading edge and secondary pulses can provide information about the true time of 
arrival of the photon. Even if the correction is small, especially in UC board, this effect should be further investigated and could lead to an improvement
of timing performances even using standard methods.
