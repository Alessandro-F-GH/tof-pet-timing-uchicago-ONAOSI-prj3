\title{\textbf{Waveform-Based Machine-Learning Timing Correction for TOF-PET Detectors}\\
\large Research Group Update}
\author{Alessandro Falcetta}
\date{09-23-2026}

\begin{document}

\maketitle
\begin{abstract}

Time-of-flight positron emission tomography (TOF-PET) benefits directly from improved coincidence timing resolution (CTR). 
This update presents a waveform-based machine-learning approach for correcting leading-edge timing using the full detector waveform.

The proposed model applies a shared detector-level scorer independently to the two aligned detector waveforms and combines the resulting scores 
antisymmetrically. The detector scorer is implemented using locally connected temporal layers, which preserve local waveform structure without imposing 
the temporal weight sharing of a convolutional neural network (CNN).

The method provides consistent CTR improvements across both front-end boards and both waveform families. Using the wider waveform window, 
the energy-channel CTR is improved by approximately \(23\)--\(34\%\) for the UC board and \(18\)--\(21\%\) for the FBK board. 
The timing-channel improvement is smaller but systematic, reaching approximately \(3\)--\(5\%\) for UC and \(5\)--\(7\%\) for FBK.

The current results are preliminary and use a fixed model configuration rather than a fully optimized hyperparameter search. 
Further gains may therefore be possible, although the present results already indicate that most of the achievable correction is captured by 
a relatively simple and structured model.

\end{abstract}
