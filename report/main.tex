\begin{abstract}
Machine-learning methods applied to detector waveforms can improve timing in time-of-flight positron emission tomography. This report investigates a detector-shared timing correction, compares it with a general joint convolutional neural network, and studies where useful timing information is located in the waveform. The framework is evaluated first on the energy channel and then on the dedicated timing channel, using blind-test coincidence timing resolution as the main quantitative metric.
