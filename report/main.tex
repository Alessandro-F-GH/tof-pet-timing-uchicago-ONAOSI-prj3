\begin{abstract}
Accurate coincidence timing is central to time-of-flight positron emission tomography.
This study describes a waveform-based correction of leading-edge timing for two
scintillation-detector modules using the same crystals and photodetectors with alternative
analog front-end electronics. Waveforms are acquired at two different stages of cascaded
amplification and used to estimate event-specific timing corrections.
The analysis examines amplification stage, detector sharing, estimator family and
waveform-window extent.
\end{abstract}
