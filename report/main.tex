\begin{abstract}
Accurate coincidence timing is central to time-of-flight positron emission tomography.
This methods draft describes a waveform-based correction of leading-edge timing for two
scintillation-detector modules equipped with alternative front-end electronics.
Detector-shared and direct estimators are evaluated within a protocol that separates
control-based preprocessing, model development and independent blind evaluation.
Timing resolution is defined by direct half-maximum interpolation of residual histograms.
The planned analysis includes model and waveform-window comparisons and temporal sensitivity
maps for each board separately. Experimental results and conclusions are reserved for completion
once the corresponding batch analyses are available.
\end{abstract}
