\section{Model architectures and training settings}
\label{app:spaces}
The following implementation details complement the model overview in
Table~\ref{tab:model-inventory}. The complete configured search spaces are
summarized in Table~\ref{tab:space-model-summary}.

\paragraph{Linear ridge regression.}
The direct estimator uses independent weights on the concatenated detector
waveforms and an intercept. The shared estimator applies common weights to the
sample-wise waveform difference, without an intercept:
\begin{equation}
h(s_1,s_2)=\mathbf{w}^{T}(s_1-s_2)
=\mathbf{w}^{T}s_1-\mathbf{w}^{T}s_2.
\end{equation}
Ridge regularization limits sensitivity to highly correlated samples and is
selected by internal leave-one-out cross-validation minimizing mean squared
error (MSE).

\paragraph{Convolutional neural networks.}
The Onishi-inspired direct CNN combines the detector dimension in the first
convolution and predicts a joint correction. The independent CNN uses
separately parameterized detector scorers, whereas the shared CNN uses one
common scorer for both detector signals. CNNs are included because temporal
filters exploit local waveform structure and have been successfully applied
to waveform timing~\cite{onishi2022,loignon2025}.

\paragraph{Multilayer perceptrons.}
The direct dense MLP performs nonlinear regression on concatenated waveforms.
The shared MLP applies the same nonlinear scorer to both detector signals and
subtracts the outputs,
\begin{equation}
h_\theta(s_1,s_2)=g_\theta(s_1)-g_\theta(s_2).
\end{equation}
The shared locally connected MLP preserves temporal neighborhoods without
sharing filters across positions, providing an intermediate assumption
between dense and convolutional processing~\cite{elsayed2020}.

\paragraph{MiniRocket feature regression.}
MiniRocket is an efficient, largely deterministic convolutional feature
transformation for time series~\cite{minirocket2021}. The direct model
transforms the waveform pair jointly; the shared model applies the same
univariate transform to both detector signals and regresses on their feature
difference.

\paragraph{Training settings.}
Dense MLPs, the locally connected MLP and temporal CNNs use at most 300 epochs,
an internal holdout fraction of 0.2 for early stopping, patience of 10 epochs,
a minimum loss improvement of $0.1\,\mathrm{ps}$, gradient clipping at 10,
and a gradient-stop threshold of $10^{-6}$ with patience 4. The
Onishi-inspired CNN uses a fixed 100-epoch schedule with learning-rate
reductions at epochs 30 and 60. Neural and MiniRocket hyperparameters are
selected using mean held-out CTR in the three common outer folds; linear ridge
uses internal leave-one-out cross-validation on MSE.
