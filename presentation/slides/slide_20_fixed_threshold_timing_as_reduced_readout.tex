\begin{frame}{Reduced readout: Pico-TDC}

\small

\begin{block}{Why compare with a reduced readout?}
The oscilloscope gives access to the complete detector waveform, while the Pico-TDC records a compact set of timing observables. The comparison tests how much timing performance can be retained when the full waveform is no longer available.
\end{block}

\begin{block}{Independent analysis path}
The waveform-ML pipeline uses only full-waveform Linear SVR and CNN models. Pico-TDC threshold and ToT studies are performed independently in \texttt{janus\_data\_analysis}; they are not an ML model family inside the waveform pipeline.
\end{block}

\begin{alertblock}{Interpretation}
This keeps the electronics comparison scientifically separate from waveform-model selection and avoids treating a reduced threshold representation as another waveform-ML candidate.
\end{alertblock}

\end{frame}
