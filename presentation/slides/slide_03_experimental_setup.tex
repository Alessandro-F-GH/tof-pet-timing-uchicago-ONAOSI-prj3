\begin{block}{Detector system}

\begin{itemize}

    \item Two opposing LYSO--SiPM detector modules.

    \item $^{22}$Na positron-emitting source centered between the two detectors.

\end{itemize}

\end{block}

\begin{alertblock}{Principle}
  A 511-keV photon produces scintillation light in the LYSO crystal, which the SiPM converts into an electrical pulse.

\end{alertblock}

\begin{block}{Analog outputs}

For both detectors:

\begin{itemize}

    \item \textbf{energy} channel: first stage of amplification. Necessary for event selection, but can also be used for timing.

    \item \textbf{timing} channel: second stage of amplification, specifically designed for timing.

\end{itemize}

\end{block}
