\begin{block}{Timing difference}
For each selected coincidence event:
\[
\Delta t = t_1-t_2 .
\]
The selected events form the coincidence time distribution.
\end{block}

\begin{block}{Coincidence Time Resolution (CTR)}
The timing histogram uses a fixed physical bin width. CTR is measured directly from the two half-maximum crossings:
\[
\CTR = t_{\mathrm{right},1/2}-t_{\mathrm{left},1/2} .
\]
The crossings are linearly interpolated between adjacent bin centres.
\end{block}
\end{column}
\end{columns}
\mainmsg{The same fixed-bin direct-FWHM definition is used for every timing method.}
